# Biomes

## Biome Groups

* **Temperate**: Moderate climate zones with seasonal variation
* **Cold**: Frozen and arctic regions
* **Hot**: Desert and volcanic environments
* **Wetland**: Water-dominated ecosystems
* **Underground**: Subterranean environments
* **Magical**: Supernaturally altered landscapes

---

## Biome List

* **Temperate: Forest**
    * Deciduous Forest: Leaf-shedding trees with seasonal changes
    * Coniferous Forest: Evergreen pine and fir woodlands
    * Mixed Forest: Combination of deciduous and evergreen trees
    * Enchanted Forest: Magical woodland with fey influence
* **Temperate: Grassland**
    * Prairie: Flat open grasslands with few trees
    * Savannah: Tropical grassland with scattered trees
    * Meadow: Flowering grass fields near civilization
    * Steppe: Dry grassland transitioning to desert
* **Cold: Arctic**
    * Tundra: Frozen treeless plains with permafrost
    * Glacier: Massive ice sheets and frozen rivers
    * Ice Caves: Frozen underground caverns
    * Polar Sea: Frozen ocean with ice floes
* **Cold: Mountain**
    * Alpine: High elevation rocky peaks
    * Snow-Capped Peaks: Permanent snow coverage
    * Mountain Pass: Traversable route through ranges
    * Highland Plateau: Elevated flat terrain
* **Hot: Desert**
    * Sandy Desert: Vast dune-covered wastelands
    * Rocky Desert: Stone and gravel badlands
    * Oasis: Isolated water source in desert
    * Canyon: Deep eroded rock formations
* **Hot: Volcanic**
    * Lava Fields: Solidified magma plains
    * Active Volcano: Erupting mountain of fire
    * Geothermal Springs: Hot water vents and geysers
    * Ash Wastes: Volcanic debris-covered land
* **Wetland: Swamp**
    * Freshwater Swamp: Tree-filled wetlands
    * Saltwater Marsh: Coastal tidal wetlands
    * Bog: Acidic peat-filled waterlogged ground
    * Mangrove: Coastal tropical wetland forest
* **Wetland: Aquatic**
    * Ocean: Deep saltwater seas
    * Coral Reef: Underwater tropical ecosystems
    * River: Flowing freshwater waterways
    * Lake: Enclosed freshwater bodies
* **Underground: Cave**
    * Limestone Caverns: Natural dissolved rock caves
    * Crystal Caves: Gem-filled underground grottos
    * Lava Tubes: Volcanic tunnel systems
    * Mushroom Grotto: Fungal-dominated caves
* **Underground: Constructed**
    * Ancient Ruins: Forgotten civilization remains
    * Mine Shafts: Excavated ore tunnels
    * Catacombs: Underground burial networks
    * Dungeon: Constructed prison complex
* **Magical: Elemental**
    * Elemental Plane of Fire: Burning dimension of flames
    * Elemental Plane of Water: Endless aquatic realm
    * Elemental Plane of Earth: Solid stone dimension
    * Elemental Plane of Air: Weightless sky realm
* **Magical: Corrupted**
    * Blighted Lands: Death-touched corrupted terrain
    * Shadow Realm: Darkness-infused parallel world
    * Twisted Forest: Mutation-warped woodland
    * Void Wastes: Reality-fragmenting emptiness
