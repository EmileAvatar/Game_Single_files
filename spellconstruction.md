# Spell Construction

## Rune System Overview

Spells are constructed by combining runes in specific slots. Each spell requires at minimum:
- **1 Element Rune**: Determines damage/effect type and base power
- **1 Shape Rune**: Defines area of effect and targeting
- **1 Effect Rune**: Determines primary magical effect (damage, heal, buff, etc.)

Optional modifiers include:
- **Augment Runes**: Enhance power, duration, or add special properties
- **Reduction Runes**: Lower mana cost or reduce casting time

**Mana Cost Formula**: (Element Cost + Shape Cost + Effect Cost + Augment Costs - Reduction Costs) × Power Multiplier

---

## Rune Categories

* **Element**: Damage type and elemental properties
* **Shape**: Area of effect and targeting pattern
* **Effect**: Primary magical function
* **Augment**: Enhancement and modification
* **Reduction**: Cost and requirement lowering

## Futhark Runes (Elder Futhark)

The 24 runes of the Elder Futhark alphabet form the foundation of runic magic. Each rune represents a concept, force, or principle that can be invoked in spellcasting. These runes can be combined with the Element, Shape, Effect, Augment, and Reduction runes to add specific properties or fulfill thematic spell requirements.

* **Futhark: Aett of Freyr (Growth & Fertility)**
    * Fehu (ᚠ): Wealth, abundance, material power - Enhances resource generation, gold finding, and prosperity effects
    * Uruz (ᚢ): Strength, vitality, primal force - Boosts physical power, health, and raw energy
    * Thurisaz (ᚦ): Thorn, defense, reactive force - Adds thorns/reflection damage, defensive counterattacks
    * Ansuz (ᚨ): Divine inspiration, communication, wisdom - Enhances divination, mental spells, and understanding
    * Raidho (ᚱ): Journey, movement, rhythm - Improves teleportation, speed buffs, and travel magic
    * Kenaz (ᚲ): Torch, knowledge, illumination - Adds fire properties, reveals hidden things, enlightens
    * Gebo (ᚷ): Gift, exchange, balance - Creates reciprocal effects, balances costs/benefits, sharing magic
    * Wunjo (ᚹ): Joy, harmony, perfection - Adds buff effects, morale boosts, positive energy

* **Futhark: Aett of Heimdall (Protection & Mystery)**
    * Hagalaz (ᚺ): Hail, destruction, uncontrolled forces - Adds ice/cold damage, chaotic effects, destructive power
    * Nauthiz (ᚾ): Need, necessity, constraint - Creates binding effects, constraints, forced actions
    * Isa (ᛁ): Ice, stillness, stasis - Freezing effects, time stop, preservation, immobilization
    * Jera (ᛃ): Year, harvest, cycles - Enhances duration, creates cyclical effects, seasonal magic
    * Eihwaz (ᛇ): Yew tree, endurance, death/rebirth - Adds necromantic power, death magic, resurrection
    * Perthro (ᛈ): Mystery, fate, hidden things - Enhances luck effects, random outcomes, fate manipulation
    * Algiz (ᛉ): Elk, protection, divine connection - Powerful defensive magic, warding, sanctuary
    * Sowilo (ᛊ): Sun, success, vital force - Adds holy/radiant damage, victory buffs, life energy

* **Futhark: Aett of Tyr (War & Order)**
    * Tiwaz (ᛏ): Tyr, justice, sacrifice - Enhances combat magic, honor-bound effects, sacrifice mechanics
    * Berkano (ᛒ): Birch, growth, nurturing - Healing enhancement, growth effects, natural magic
    * Ehwaz (ᛖ): Horse, movement, partnership - Companion/mount buffs, teamwork bonuses, speed
    * Mannaz (ᛗ): Humanity, intelligence, social order - Social magic, mind effects, civilization-themed spells
    * Laguz (ᛚ): Water, flow, intuition - Water/fluid magic, adaptability, emotional/psychic power
    * Ingwaz (ᛜ): Fertility, potential, gestation - Creates seeds of power, delayed effects, potential energy
    * Dagaz (ᛞ): Day, breakthrough, transformation - Adds transformation effects, breakthroughs, sudden changes
    * Othala (ᛟ): Homeland, inheritance, tradition - Ancestral magic, legacy effects, territorial power

* **Futhark: Usage in Spells**
    * Thematic Enhancement: Adding relevant Futhark rune strengthens spell concept (Kenaz + Fire = more potent)
    * Rune Words: Combining 2-3 Futhark runes creates powerful symbolic effects
    * Mana Modification: Each Futhark rune adds +5 mana cost but +10% effectiveness in its domain
    * Stability Bonus: Thematically appropriate Futhark runes improve spell stability by 15%
    * Cultural Magic: Different cultures/factions may require specific Futhark runes for their spells

* **Futhark: Rune Word Examples**
    * Kenaz + Sowilo (Torch + Sun): Double light/fire potency, +30% fire damage
    * Isa + Hagalaz (Ice + Hail): Ultimate frozen destruction, +40% ice damage, guaranteed slow
    * Algiz + Tiwaz (Protection + Justice): Righteous defense, damage reflection for allies
    * Eihwaz + Othala (Death + Homeland): Ancestral guardian summoning, undead protectors
    * Raidho + Ehwaz (Journey + Horse): Superior travel magic, teleportation with companions
    * Ansuz + Perthro (Wisdom + Mystery): Perfect divination, reveals all secrets
    * Uruz + Thurisaz (Strength + Thorn): Unstoppable force, breaking through all barriers
    * Wunjo + Gebo (Joy + Gift): Mass blessing, party-wide enhancement with no downside

## Element Runes

* **Element: Primary**
    * Fire Rune: Burning damage with ignite chance (Base: 15 mana, 1.2x multiplier)
    * Ice Rune: Freezing damage with slow effect (Base: 15 mana, 1.0x multiplier)
    * Lightning Rune: Electrical damage with chain potential (Base: 18 mana, 1.3x multiplier)
    * Acid Rune: Corrosive damage with armor reduction (Base: 16 mana, 1.1x multiplier)
    * Force Rune: Pure magical energy (Base: 20 mana, 1.4x multiplier)
    * Shadow Rune: Dark energy with accuracy penalty (Base: 17 mana, 1.2x multiplier)
* **Element: Advanced**
    * Holy Rune: Radiant damage bonus vs undead/demons (Base: 22 mana, 1.5x multiplier)
    * Necrotic Rune: Death energy with life drain (Base: 25 mana, 1.6x multiplier)
    * Poison Rune: Toxic damage over time (Base: 14 mana, 0.9x multiplier, 5 turn DOT)
    * Nature Rune: Natural magic with entangle chance (Base: 16 mana, 1.1x multiplier)
    * Psychic Rune: Mental damage ignoring physical armor (Base: 24 mana, 1.5x multiplier)
    * Void Rune: Reality-warping energy with dispel effect (Base: 30 mana, 1.8x multiplier)
* **Element: Combination**
    * Steam Rune (Fire + Ice): Scalding vapor dealing hybrid damage (Base: 28 mana, 1.4x multiplier)
    * Magma Rune (Fire + Earth): Molten rock with knockback (Base: 32 mana, 1.6x multiplier)
    * Storm Rune (Lightning + Air): Thunderous blast with stun (Base: 35 mana, 1.7x multiplier)
    * Blood Rune (Shadow + Life): Sacrificial magic using HP for power (Base: 20 mana, 2.0x multiplier, costs 15% max HP)
    * Radiant Rune (Holy + Fire): Purifying flames (Base: 40 mana, 1.9x multiplier)
    * Chaos Rune (All Elements): Random elemental effect (Base: 50 mana, 2.2x multiplier)

## Shape Runes

* **Shape: Single Target**
    * Dart Rune: Single target, long range projectile (Base: 5 mana, +0 multiplier)
    * Touch Rune: Melee range single target (Base: 2 mana, +0.2x multiplier)
    * Beam Rune: Single target piercing ray (Base: 8 mana, +0.1x multiplier)
    * Seeking Rune: Auto-tracking projectile (Base: 12 mana, +0 multiplier, never misses)
* **Shape: Area Effect**
    * Nova Rune: 1x1 radius centered on target (Base: 8 mana, +0.3x multiplier)
    * Blast Rune: 2x2 radius explosion (Base: 15 mana, +0.5x multiplier)
    * Eruption Rune: 3x3 radius large explosion (Base: 25 mana, +0.7x multiplier)
    * Cataclysm Rune: 4x4 radius devastating blast (Base: 40 mana, +0.9x multiplier)
    * Apocalypse Rune: 5x5 radius ultimate explosion (Base: 60 mana, +1.2x multiplier)
* **Shape: Linear**
    * Line Rune: Straight line 8 tiles long (Base: 10 mana, +0.4x multiplier)
    * Cone Rune: 90-degree frontal arc (Base: 12 mana, +0.5x multiplier)
    * Wave Rune: 180-degree semicircle wave (Base: 20 mana, +0.7x multiplier)
* **Shape: Special**
    * Chain Rune: Bounces between 3-5 targets (Base: 18 mana, +0.6x multiplier, 80% damage per bounce)
    * Wall Rune: Creates 5-tile barrier (Base: 15 mana, +0.4x multiplier, persists 3 turns)
    * Rain Rune: Random strikes in 3x3 area (Base: 22 mana, +0.8x multiplier, 5-8 hits)
    * Field Rune: Ground effect lasting multiple turns (Base: 20 mana, +0.3x multiplier, persists 5 turns)
* **Shape: Support**
    * Self Rune: Affects only caster (Base: 0 mana, +0 multiplier)
    * Ally Rune: Single friendly target (Base: 3 mana, +0 multiplier)
    * Party Rune: All party members (Base: 15 mana, +0.6x multiplier)
    * Aura Rune: 2x2 radius centered on caster (Base: 12 mana, +0.4x multiplier, moves with caster)

## Effect Runes

* **Effect: Damage**
    * Strike Rune: Direct damage dealing (Base: 10 mana, standard damage formula)
    * Pierce Rune: Ignores 30% of resistances (Base: 15 mana, +0.3x multiplier)
    * Crush Rune: Extra damage vs armored targets (Base: 12 mana, +0.2x multiplier)
    * Shatter Rune: Bonus damage vs shields/barriers (Base: 14 mana, +0.25x multiplier)
* **Effect: Healing**
    * Restore Rune: Heals target for moderate amount (Base: 12 mana, heals 40-60 HP)
    * Regenerate Rune: Heal over time effect (Base: 15 mana, heals 15 HP/turn for 5 turns)
    * Revive Rune: Resurrects fallen ally (Base: 50 mana, restores to 50% HP)
    * Cleanse Rune: Removes negative status effects (Base: 20 mana)
* **Effect: Buff**
    * Empower Rune: Increases damage output (Base: 15 mana, +30% damage for 5 turns)
    * Fortify Rune: Increases defense and resistances (Base: 15 mana, +25% resistance for 5 turns)
    * Quicken Rune: Increases speed and action points (Base: 18 mana, +1 AP for 5 turns)
    * Sharpen Rune: Increases accuracy and critical chance (Base: 14 mana, +15% hit/crit for 5 turns)
* **Effect: Debuff**
    * Weaken Rune: Reduces target damage output (Base: 12 mana, -30% damage for 5 turns)
    * Slow Rune: Reduces movement and action points (Base: 14 mana, -1 AP for 5 turns)
    * Blind Rune: Reduces accuracy drastically (Base: 15 mana, -50% hit chance for 4 turns)
    * Silence Rune: Prevents spellcasting (Base: 20 mana, 3 turn duration)
    * Curse Rune: General combat penalty (Base: 16 mana, -20% all stats for 5 turns)
* **Effect: Control**
    * Stun Rune: Immobilizes target completely (Base: 25 mana, 1-2 turn stun)
    * Fear Rune: Forces target to flee (Base: 18 mana, 3 turn flee)
    * Charm Rune: Makes enemy temporarily friendly (Base: 30 mana, 5 turn charm)
    * Confuse Rune: Causes random actions (Base: 20 mana, 4 turn confusion)
    * Dominate Rune: Direct mind control (Base: 45 mana, 3 turn control)
* **Effect: Summoning**
    * Conjure Rune: Summons basic creature (Base: 25 mana, lasts until defeated)
    * Bind Rune: Summons powerful entity (Base: 50 mana, lasts 10 turns or until defeated)
    * Gate Rune: Opens portal for extra-planar being (Base: 80 mana, lasts until defeated)
* **Effect: Utility**
    * Dispel Rune: Removes magical effects (Base: 18 mana, removes up to 3 effects)
    * Shield Rune: Creates protective barrier (Base: 15 mana, absorbs 50-80 damage)
    * Detect Rune: Reveals hidden things (Base: 10 mana, reveals invisibility/traps in 5x5 area)
    * Teleport Rune: Instant relocation (Base: 30 mana, range 10 tiles)
    * Transform Rune: Alters physical form (Base: 35 mana, polymorphs for 10 turns)

## Augment Runes

* **Augment: Power**
    * Amplify Rune: +40% effect magnitude (Cost: +12 mana, +0.4x multiplier)
    * Maximize Rune: +80% effect magnitude (Cost: +25 mana, +0.8x multiplier)
    * Overload Rune: +150% effect magnitude (Cost: +50 mana, +1.5x multiplier, 20% backfire chance)
* **Augment: Duration**
    * Extend Rune: +50% duration (Cost: +8 mana)
    * Prolong Rune: +100% duration (Cost: +15 mana)
    * Perpetuate Rune: +200% duration (Cost: +30 mana)
* **Augment: Range**
    * Reach Rune: +50% range (Cost: +6 mana)
    * Distance Rune: +100% range (Cost: +12 mana)
    * Horizon Rune: +200% range (Cost: +25 mana)
* **Augment: Penetration**
    * Breach Rune: Ignore 25% resistance (Cost: +10 mana)
    * Rend Rune: Ignore 50% resistance (Cost: +20 mana)
    * Annihilate Rune: Ignore 75% resistance (Cost: +35 mana)
* **Augment: Special**
    * Split Rune: +1 additional projectile (Cost: +15 mana, each projectile at 70% power)
    * Echo Rune: Spell triggers twice (Cost: +40 mana, second cast at 60% power)
    * Persist Rune: Creates lingering field effect (Cost: +18 mana, persists 3 turns)
    * Lifesteal Rune: Converts 25% damage to healing (Cost: +16 mana)
    * Critical Rune: +25% critical strike chance (Cost: +12 mana, critical deals 2x damage)
    * Pierce All Rune: Spell affects allies and enemies (Cost: +8 mana, removes friendly fire protection)

## Reduction Runes

* **Reduction: Cost**
    * Efficient Rune: -15% total mana cost (Requires: Intelligence 15+)
    * Economic Rune: -30% total mana cost (Requires: Intelligence 20+)
    * Frugal Rune: -50% total mana cost (Requires: Intelligence 25+, +2 turn cast time)
* **Reduction: Casting**
    * Swift Rune: -1 turn casting time (Cost: +10 mana, minimum 1 turn)
    * Instant Rune: Cast as reaction/interrupt (Cost: +25 mana)
    * Ritual Rune: +3 turn casting time for -40% mana cost (Requires: Spellcraft 15+)
* **Reduction: Requirements**
    * Simplified Rune: -5 skill requirement (Cost: +8 mana, -15% effectiveness)
    * Universal Rune: No skill requirement (Cost: +20 mana, -30% effectiveness)

## Spell Discovery System

* **Discovery: Learning Methods**
    * Rune Finding: Discover individual runes in world
    * Spell Scroll: Learn complete spell formula
    * Experimentation: Combine known runes to create new spells
    * Research: Study magic to unlock advanced runes
    * Teaching: Learn from NPC mages and trainers

* **Discovery: Rune Acquisition**
    * Common Runes: Basic elements, shapes, effects available at start
    * Uncommon Runes: Found in dungeons, purchased from vendors
    * Rare Runes: Quest rewards, boss drops, hidden locations
    * Legendary Runes: Ultimate combinations, research unlocks, major achievements

* **Discovery: Spell Saving**
    * Spellbook Slots: Limited number of constructed spells
    * Spell Names: Players name their creations
    * Quick Cast: Saved spells cast without reconstruction
    * Spell Sharing: Trade spell formulas with other players

## Advanced Mechanics

* **Mechanics: Spell Stability**
    * Stability Rating: Based on rune compatibility
    * Compatible Runes: Reduce mana cost 5-15%
    * Incompatible Runes: Increase mana cost 10-30%, add backfire risk
    * Example Compatibility: Fire + Lightning = Incompatible (explosive combination)
    * Example Compatibility: Ice + Water = Compatible (natural synergy)

* **Mechanics: Rune Slots**
    * Minimum: 3 runes (Element + Shape + Effect)
    * Maximum: 8 runes (3 required + 5 optional)
    * Slot Types: Must include at least one of each required type
    * Augment Limit: Maximum 3 augment runes per spell
    * Reduction Limit: Maximum 2 reduction runes per spell

* **Mechanics: Power Scaling**
    * Caster Level: Adds +2% damage/healing per level
    * Relevant Stat: Intelligence/Wisdom adds +1% per 2 points
    * Skill Bonus: Magic school skill adds +3% per 5 points
    * Equipment: Staves, wands, orbs provide bonuses

* **Mechanics: Critical Strikes**
    * Base Crit Chance: 5%
    * Crit Multiplier: 2x damage/healing
    * Crit Effects: Status effects have double duration on crit
    * Crit Augments: Critical Rune increases chance

* **Mechanics: Resistance & Penetration**
    * Enemy Resistance: Reduces damage by percentage
    * Armor: Physical reduction doesn't affect most magic
    * Element-Specific: Fire resist vs fire spells, etc.
    * Penetration: Pierce, Breach, Rend, Annihilate runes bypass resistance

* **Mechanics: Spell Failure**
    * Silence Effect: Cannot cast any spells
    * Insufficient Mana: Spell fizzles with no effect
    * Concentration Break: Damage during casting interrupts spell
    * Backfire: Overload and unstable combinations risk damaging caster
    * Anti-Magic Zones: Certain areas prevent spellcasting
