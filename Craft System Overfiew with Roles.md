Craft System
 - Weapon Crafting groups: Swords, Daggers, Hammers, Arrows
 - Armour Crafting groups: Armour, helmet, Gloves, Cloaks, Belts, Boots
 - Jewelry Crafting groups: Amulets, Rings, Crowns
 - Potions Crafting groups: Regents, Potions, Alchemy
 - Magic Crafting groups: Mage Spells, Priests Spells, Elemental Spells
 - Harvest Crafting groups: Mining, Herb collection, Farming, Animal processing, Monster processing. eg skinning and cutting monster/animals to get parts
 - Furniture Crafting groups: tables, chairs, walls, doors, traps, roofs, decorations, kitchen, workbenches, Mining Equipment, Farming Equipment, Alchemy Equipment
 - Monster Crafting groups: Animal Breeding, Monster Breeding, Monster Creation, Geneforge Creation 

 Player Role: 
 Slime Architect: Mage to Raise and monsters to hunt people and raid human settlements. Specific using slimes. note creating a separate game for this one 
 Monster Forge: Create various monster to hunt people and raid human settlements. Using various Monsters. 
 Alchemist: Create various potions and alchemy items, Magic items 
 Blacksmith: Creates various items from the first list. Hunter: Hunts Monsters, sells the monster parts to Alchemists and Blacksmits
