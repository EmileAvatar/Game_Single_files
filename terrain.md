# Terrain

## Terrain Groups

* **Ground: Natural**: Basic outdoor surface types
* **Ground: Constructed**: Man-made floor surfaces
* **Ground: Hazardous**: Dangerous ground terrain
* **Wall: Natural**: Natural barrier formations
* **Wall: Constructed**: Built barrier structures
* **Wall: Special**: Magical or unique barriers
* **Water**: Liquid terrain types
* **Vegetation**: Plant-covered surfaces
* **Underground**: Subterranean terrain
* **Special**: Unique magical or environmental terrain
