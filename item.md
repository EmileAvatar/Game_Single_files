# Items

## Item Group Types

* **Weapons**: Melee and ranged tools for combat
     * Blade: Sharp-edged weapons for cutting and stabbing
     * Blunt: Heavy weapons for crushing and bashing
     * Pole: Long-handled weapons for reach advantage
     * Ranged: Projectile weapons for distance combat
     * Throwing: Balanced weapons for hurling at enemies
* **Armor**: Protective gear for body, head, and extremities
     * Body: Torso protection from light to heavy
     * Head: Helmets and caps protecting the skull
     * Hands: Gloves and gauntlets for hand protection
     * Feet: Boots and footwear for mobility and defense
     * Shield: Blocking tools for active defense
     * Legs: Greaves and leg armor
     * Robe: Magical garments for spellcasters
     * Cloak: Outer garments for protection and utility
     * Belt: Waist equipment for storage and enhancement
     * Bracers: Forearm protection and combat aids
     * Accessories: Rings, necklaces, piercings, and prosthetics
* **Consumables**: Single-use items like potions and scrolls
     * Potions: Liquid elixirs with immediate effects
     * Scrolls: Single-use spell parchments
     * Flasks: Throwable liquid containers
     * Blessed Items: Holy water and purifying substances
* **Artifacts**: Unique magical items with special abilities
     * Jewelry: Rings, amulets, and pendants
     * Worn Items: Boots, belts, and gauntlets
     * Held Items: Orbs and crowns
* **Crafting Materials**: Resources used to create or enhance items
     * Ores: Metal-bearing rocks and minerals
     * Refined Metals: Processed ingots and alloys
     * Organic: Leather, wood, and plant materials
     * Magical: Essences and crystals for enchanting
     * Stone: Building blocks and clay
     * Alchemical: Compounds for potion-making
     * Building Materials: Bricks, mortar, nails, hinges, glass, doors, windows
* **Magical Essences**: Mystical power sources for magic item creation, creature crafting, lair power stations, and high-level spells
     * Life Force: Creature blood and vitality essences
     * Arcane Power: Raw and refined magical energy
     * Creation Force: Fundamental energies for spawning life
     * Divine: Sacred god-blood and holy powers
     * Celestial: Astronomical and cosmic essences
* **Herbs**: Plant materials harvested for alchemical processing and essence extraction
     * Healing: Restorative plants for health potions
     * Stamina: Energy-boosting plants for endurance
     * Mental: Mind-affecting herbs for clarity and mana
     * Elemental: Magically-attuned plants aligned to elements
     * Toxic: Poisonous plants for venoms and dangerous compounds
     * Rare: Exotic and legendary botanical specimens
* **Storage Containers**: Vessels for storing materials, essences, and finished products
     * Basic Storage: Simple racks and mundane containers
     * Alchemical Storage: Specialized vials and bottles for reagents
     * Magical Storage: Enchanted containers for volatile substances
     * Specialized Storage: Rune-etched vessels for powerful essences
* **Quest Items**: Special objects required for story progression
     * Keys: Unlocking specific locations
     * Documents: Letters and seals
     * Artifacts: Sacred relics and medallions
     * Building: Permits, deeds, and architectural plans
* **Tools**: Utility items for exploration and interaction
     * Lockpicking: Tools for opening locks
     * Climbing: Ropes and grappling hooks
     * Light: Lanterns and torches
     * Survival: Tents, water skins, and fishing gear
     * Excavation: Shovels and digging implements
     * Navigation: Compass and maps
     * Construction: Masonry and surveying tools
     * Defenses: Traps, wards, and security systems
* **Craft Stations**: Fixed installations for crafting specific item types
     * Forges: Metal working and weapon creation
     * Tables: Alchemy and enchanting workstations
     * Processing: Tanning, weaving, and woodworking
     * Creation: Vats and chambers for living creatures
* **Organic Parts**: Monster harvested components for alchemy and creation
     * Brains: Neural tissue for intelligence
     * Eyes: Visual organs for perception abilities
     * Horns: Keratin growths for weapons and armor
     * Claws: Sharp talons for cutting
     * Hides: Skins and scales for armor
     * Organs: Hearts, glands, and internal components
     * Wings: Flight appendages
     * Bones: Skeletal materials
     * Fluids: Blood, venom, and other liquids
* **Crystals**: Precious gems for enchanting, jewelry, and magical focuses
     * Precious: Ruby, diamond, sapphire, emerald
     * Semi-Precious: Amethyst, topaz, garnet, jade
     * Quartz: Clear, rose, smoky varieties
     * Specialty: Moonstone, sunstone, opal
     * Rare: Prismatic shard, voidstone, star sapphire
* **MagicTech**: Laboratory equipment for researching spells, monsters, and magical innovations
     * Extractors: Devices harvesting essences and energy
     * Analyzers: Instruments measuring magical properties
     * Chambers: Controlled environments for experiments
     * Storage: Arrays and archives for data
     * Manipulation: Looms, crucibles, and splicing tables
     * Observation: Scrying pools and observatories
     * Containment: Fields and circles for dangerous work
* **Industry**: Machines and equipment for building settlements, lairs, and laboratories
     * Lifting: Cranes and pulleys for moving heavy materials
     * Transport: Carts, rails, and conveyors for material movement
     * Mining: Equipment for extracting resources from earth
     * Construction: Scaffolding and support structures
     * Processing: Mills and refineries for raw materials
     * Power: Waterwheels and windmills for mechanical energy
     * Storage: Silos and warehouses for bulk materials
* **Buildings/Structures**: Placeable building tiles and components for construction
     * Walls: Vertical barriers and structural elements
     * Floors: Ground surfaces and pavement
     * Openings: Doors, windows, and gates
     * Infrastructure: Roads, bridges, and pathways
     * Agriculture: Farm plots and growing areas
     * Fortifications: Defensive walls and battlements
* **Furniture**: Interior items and decorative elements
     * Seating: Chairs, benches, and thrones
     * Storage: Chests, shelves, and cabinets
     * Work Surfaces: Tables, desks, and benches
     * Sleeping: Beds and bedrolls
     * Display: Weapon racks, armor stands, and pedestals
     * Decoration: Banners, statues, paintings, and trophies
     * Creature Care: Spawning pools, feeding troughs, training circles
* **Arcane Spell Focus**: Magical implements for storing and channeling spells (see arcane_spell_focus.md for full details)
     * Books: Standard books, pocket books, and heavy tomes for spell storage
     * Crystal Balls: Scrying orbs and specialized elemental crystals
     * Staves: Weapon-implement hybrids for combat mages
     * Amulets: Neck-worn magical jewelry for spell storage
     * Rings: Finger-worn bands for quick-cast spells
     * Rune Tattoos: Permanent body inscriptions with enhanced spell power
* **Restraints**: Equipment for controlling and containing creatures and captives
     * Collars: Slave collars and control devices
     * Harnesses: Mount control systems for horses and beasts
     * Chains: Heavy links for securing doors, gates, and prisoners
     * Cages: Small, medium, and large containment structures
