# Spells

## Spell Groups

* **Combat: Direct Damage**: Offensive spells dealing immediate harm
* **Combat: Area Effect**: Spells affecting multiple targets or zones
* **Combat: Debuff**: Weakening and hindering enemy capabilities
* **Healing**: Restoration of health and vitality
* **Protection**: Defensive magic and damage prevention
* **Summoning**: Calling creatures and conjuring allies
* **Enchantment: Buff**: Enhancing ally capabilities
* **Enchantment: Mind**: Controlling thoughts and emotions
* **Divination**: Revealing hidden information and future events
* **Illusion**: Creating false perceptions and deceptions
* **Transmutation**: Altering physical properties and forms
* **Necromancy**: Death magic and life force manipulation
* **Nature**: Elemental and plant-based magic
* **Utility**: Practical non-combat applications

---

## Area of Effect Types

* **Self**: Affects only the caster
* **Single Target**: Affects one chosen creature or object
* **Touch**: Requires physical contact with target
* **Line**: Affects all in straight path
* **Cone**: Affects frontal arc area
* **1x1 Area**: Single tile radius explosion
* **2x2 Area**: Small radius explosion
* **3x3 Area**: Medium radius explosion
* **4x4 Area**: Large radius explosion
* **5x5 Area**: Huge radius explosion
* **Item Imbue**: Enchants object with spell effect
* **Weapon Enchant**: Adds magical property to weapon
* **Armor Enchant**: Adds magical property to armor
* **Party**: Affects all party members
* **All Allies**: Affects all friendly creatures in range
* **All Enemies**: Affects all hostile creatures in range
* **Global**: Affects entire battlefield or map
