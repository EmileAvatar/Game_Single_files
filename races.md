# Races

## Race Groups

* **Standard**: Common civilized races found in most settings
* **Exotic**: Rare or unusual playable races
* **Monstrous**: Traditionally hostile races adapted for player use
* **Elemental**: Beings infused with elemental essence
* **Hybrid**: Mixed heritage races combining traits

---

## Race List

* **Standard: Human Variants**
    * Human: Versatile adaptable race with balanced attributes and bonus skill points
    * Variant Human: Customizable human with feat selection and flexible ability scores
    * Half-Elf: Human-elf hybrid with charm, versatility, and darkvision
    * Half-Orc: Human-orc hybrid with strength, endurance, and relentless survival
* **Standard: Classical**
    * Elf: Graceful long-lived beings with magic affinity and keen senses
    * Dwarf: Sturdy underground dwellers with resilience and crafting expertise
    * Halfling: Small lucky folk with stealth and brave hearts
    * Gnome: Tiny clever tinkerers with illusion magic and curiosity
* **Standard: Elven Subraces**
    * High Elf: Intellectual magic-wielding elves with weapon training
    * Wood Elf: Forest-dwelling hunters with superior speed and camouflage
    * Dark Elf (Drow): Underground elves with darkness magic and light sensitivity
    * Sea Elf: Aquatic elves with swimming speed and underwater breathing
    * Snow Elf: Arctic-adapted elves with cold resistance and ice affinity
* **Standard: Dwarven Subraces**
    * Mountain Dwarf: Strong warriors with armor proficiency and toughness
    * Hill Dwarf: Hardy settlers with extra health and wisdom
    * Deep Dwarf (Duergar): Gray-skinned underdark dwellers with size-changing magic
    * Fire Dwarf: Volcano-dwelling smiths with fire resistance
* **Standard: Halfling Subraces**
    * Lightfoot Halfling: Stealthy social halflings who hide easily
    * Stout Halfling: Resilient hardy halflings with poison resistance
    * Tallfellow Halfling: Diplomatic inquisitive halflings with ranger skills
* **Standard: Gnome Subraces**
    * Rock Gnome: Inventive tinkerers with artifice skills and construct knowledge
    * Forest Gnome: Nature-loving gnomes with animal speech and stealth
    * Deep Gnome (Svirfneblin): Underground gnomes with stone magic and darkvision

* **Exotic: Bestial**
    * Dragonborn: Dragon-blooded humanoids with breath weapons and scaled skin
    * Tiefling: Demon-touched humans with infernal magic and dark charm
    * Aasimar: Angel-blooded humans with radiant damage and healing light
    * Tabaxi: Cat-folk with climbing speed and feline agility
    * Kenku: Crow-people with mimicry and forgery skills but cannot speak originally
    * Aarakocra: Bird-people with flight and aerial combat prowess
    * Lizardfolk: Reptilian survivalists with natural armor and swimming
    * Tortle: Turtle-folk with high natural armor and strength
    * Minotaur: Bull-headed warriors with charging attacks and horns
    * Centaur: Horse-bodied beings with speed and survival skills
    * Satyr: Goat-legged fey with magic resistance and performance skills
* **Exotic: Planar**
    * Genasi (Air): Air-touched humans with levitation and wind affinity
    * Genasi (Earth): Earth-touched humans with stone resilience and ground passage
    * Genasi (Fire): Fire-touched humans with flame resistance and burning magic
    * Genasi (Water): Water-touched humans with swimming and acid resistance
    * Shadar-Kai: Shadow-plane elves with teleportation and necrotic resistance
    * Eladrin: Feywild elves with seasonal magic and fey step teleportation
    * Gith (Githyanki): Astral-dwelling warriors with psychic resistance and silver swords
    * Gith (Githzerai): Monk-like psionicists with mental discipline and psychic defense
* **Exotic: Fey-Touched**
    * Fairy: Tiny winged fey with flight and enchantment magic
    * Harengon: Rabbit-folk with luck, jumping, and initiative bonuses
    * Owlin: Owl-humanoids with flight and silent movement
    * Sprite: Diminutive nature spirits with invisibility and heart-reading

* **Monstrous: Goblinoid**
    * Goblin: Small cunning creatures with stealth and nimbleness
    * Hobgoblin: Disciplined military goblinoids with armor proficiency and tactics
    * Bugbear: Large stealthy ambushers with reach and surprise attack damage
* **Monstrous: Orc-Kin**
    * Orc: Brutal warriors with aggression and powerful attacks
    * Half-Orc: See Standard (listed above for reference)
* **Monstrous: Underdark**
    * Drow: See Elven Subraces (listed above for reference)
    * Duergar: See Dwarven Subraces (listed above for reference)
    * Kobold: Small dragon-servants with pack tactics and trap expertise
    * Troglodyte: Primitive reptilians with stench and chameleon skin
* **Monstrous: Aberrant**
    * Mind Flayer (Illithid): Tentacled psionic brain-eaters with telepathy and mind blast
    * Beholder-Kin: Eye-stalk humanoids with magical eye rays
    * Yuan-Ti Pureblood: Snake-blooded humans with poison immunity and suggestion magic
    * Grell: Floating brain-creatures with tentacles and paralysis

* **Elemental: Fire-Born**
    * Phoenix-Touched: Rebirth-blessed humans with fire immunity and resurrection
    * Ifrit: Fire genie descendants with flame magic and heat vision
    * Magma Dwarf: Lava-infused dwarves with molten resistance
* **Elemental: Water-Born**
    * Marid-Blooded: Water genie descendants with water breathing and ice magic
    * Merfolk: Fish-tailed aquatic people with swimming and underwater adaptation
    * Triton: Noble sea-guardians with water control and aquatic allies
* **Elemental: Earth-Born**
    * Goliath: Stone-skinned giants with powerful build and mountain endurance
    * Dao-Touched: Earth genie descendants with stone shape and tremorsense
    * Crystal-Born: Gem-infused humanoids with hardened skin and reflection magic
* **Elemental: Air-Born**
    * Djinni-Blooded: Air genie descendants with wind magic and levitation
    * Cloud Giant-Kin: Sky-dwelling giants with storm affinity
    * Sylph: Air spirits with permanent flight and lightning resistance

* **Hybrid: Beast Combinations**
    * Grung: Poison frog-people with toxic skin and jumping
    * Bullywug: Frog-folk with swimming and swamp adaptation
    * Loxodon: Elephant-people with natural armor and powerful build
    * Leonin: Lion-folk with roar abilities and pride tactics
    * Hadozee: Gliding monkey-people with climbing and dexterity
* **Hybrid: Magical Fusions**
    * Warforged: Living constructs with integrated armor and no sleep requirement
    * Simic Hybrid: Magically modified humans with animal adaptations
    * Reborn: Undead-touched living with death resistance and past life memories
    * Dhampir: Half-vampire with bite attack and spider climb
    * Hexblood: Hag-cursed beings with disguise magic and hex spells
* **Hybrid: Cosmic**
    * Kalashtar: Dream-spirit bonded humans with psychic abilities and telepathy
    * Giff: Hippo-folk with gunpowder affinity and military discipline
    * Plasmoid: Ooze-people with shape-shifting and acid resistance
    * Thri-Kreen: Insectoid four-armed hunters with natural weapons and telepathy
    * Autognome: Mechanical gnome constructs with tool proficiency and damage resistance
