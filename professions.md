# Professions

## Profession Groups

* **Crafting**: Skilled artisans who create and repair items
* **Commerce**: Traders and merchants managing goods and services
* **Military**: Warriors and soldiers protecting and enforcing order
* **Governance**: Rulers and officials managing territories and laws
* **Guild**: Organized trade specialists and their hierarchies
* **Magic**: Practitioners of arcane and divine arts
* **Religion**: Clergy and faithful serving the gods
* **Service**: Workers providing essential community services
* **Criminal**: Outlaws and underground operatives
* **Entertainment**: Performers and those providing leisure
* **Scholarly**: Learned individuals pursuing knowledge
* **Exploration**: Adventurers and frontier specialists

---

## Profession List

* **Crafting: Metalworking**
    * Blacksmith: General metal worker forging tools, horseshoes, and basic weapons
    * Weaponsmith: Specialized craftsman creating quality melee weapons and ammunition
    * Armorsmith: Expert metal worker crafting protective armor and shields
    * Mage Smith: Enchanter-blacksmith forging magical weapons and rune-inscribed armor
    * Silversmith: Precious metal artisan creating jewelry and silver items
    * Goldsmith: Master craftsman working with gold for jewelry and artifacts
    * Locksmith: Security specialist crafting and picking locks
    * Tinker: General metalworker repairing tools and creating small mechanisms
    * Gunsmith: Specialist creating firearms and ammunition (if setting appropriate)
* **Crafting: Leather and Cloth**
    * Leatherworker: Craftsman treating hides and creating leather armor and goods
    * Tanner: Hide processor converting raw skins into usable leather
    * Tailor: Cloth worker sewing garments and robes
    * Weaver: Textile creator making cloth from thread and fiber
    * Cobbler: Shoemaker crafting boots and footwear
    * Furrier: Specialist working with pelts and fur garments
    * Seamstress: Clothing repair and alteration specialist
* **Crafting: Alchemy and Magic**
    * Alchemist: Potion brewer mixing elixirs, potions, and alchemical compounds
    * Herbalist: Plant expert gathering and preparing medicinal herbs
    * Poisoner: Dark craftsman creating toxins and venoms
    * Enchanter: Magical artisan imbuing items with arcane properties
    * Scroll Scribe: Magic worker inscribing spells onto parchment
    * Rune Carver: Specialist etching magical symbols into materials
    * Gem Cutter: Precision worker cutting and polishing magical crystals
* **Crafting: Wood and Stone**
    * Carpenter: Woodworker building furniture and structures
    * Woodcarver: Artistic worker creating decorative wooden items
    * Fletcher: Arrow and bow crafter specializing in ranged ammunition
    * Cooper: Barrel maker crafting containers and casks
    * Mason: Stone worker building walls and structures
    * Stonemason: Artistic stone carver creating monuments and sculptures
    * Sculptor: Artist creating statues and decorative stonework
    * Glazier: Glass worker crafting windows and glass items
* **Crafting: Specialized**
    * Jeweler: Precious stone setter creating rings, amulets, and necklaces
    * Cartographer: Map maker charting territories and dungeons
    * Scribe: Document copier and letter writer
    * Bookbinder: Book assembler creating tomes and journals
    * Candlemaker: Wax worker creating light sources
    * Soapmaker: Craftsman creating cleaning and bathing supplies
    * Brewer: Ale and beer creator running fermentation operations
    * Vintner: Wine maker and grape grower
    * Distiller: Spirit creator making strong alcoholic beverages
    * Perfumer: Scent creator mixing aromatic compounds

* **Commerce: Shops**
    * Shopkeeper: General store owner selling varied goods
    * Merchant: Traveling trader buying and selling across regions
    * Trader: Market stall operator exchanging goods
    * Vendor: Street seller offering specific wares
    * Peddler: Wandering salesman carrying goods to remote areas
    * Fence: Black market dealer buying and selling stolen goods
    * Appraiser: Value expert identifying and pricing rare items
    * Auctioneer: Bid caller selling items to highest bidder
* **Commerce: Services**
    * Innkeeper: Lodging provider offering rooms and meals
    * Tavern Keeper: Drink establishment owner serving alcohol and food
    * Barkeep: Server and bartender managing bar operations
    * Banker: Money keeper managing deposits and loans
    * Moneylender: Loan provider charging interest
    * Tax Collector: Crown agent gathering revenues
    * Customs Officer: Border agent inspecting goods and collecting tariffs
    * Quartermaster: Supply manager distributing equipment and provisions
* **Commerce: Transport**
    * Caravan Master: Trade convoy leader organizing merchant expeditions
    * Ship Captain: Vessel commander managing maritime trade
    * Ferryman: River crossing operator transporting people and goods
    * Stable Master: Horse keeper renting mounts and storage
    * Wagon Driver: Transport operator hauling cargo

* **Military: Infantry**
    * Guard: City protector maintaining order and security
    * City Guard: Urban patrol officer enforcing local laws
    * Town Guard: Settlement defender protecting smaller communities
    * Gate Guard: Entry point sentry controlling access
    * Royal Guard: Elite palace protector serving nobility
    * Bodyguard: Personal protector hired for individual safety
    * Soldier: Professional warrior serving in armies
    * Footman: Basic infantry fighter
    * Pikeman: Pole-weapon specialist in formation combat
    * Archer: Ranged combatant using bows
    * Crossbowman: Heavy ranged fighter with crossbows
* **Military: Cavalry and Elite**
    * Knight: Noble warrior with armor and code of honor
    * Cavalier: Mounted warrior specializing in horseback combat
    * Lancer: Mounted charge specialist with lance weapons
    * Cataphract: Heavy armored cavalry elite
    * Paladin: Holy warrior combining combat and divine magic
    * Champion: Elite combatant and tournament fighter
    * Templar: Religious military order knight
* **Military: Officers**
    * Captain: Unit commander leading squads and companies
    * Lieutenant: Second-in-command assisting captains
    * Sergeant: Non-commissioned officer training troops
    * General: Army commander managing military strategy
    * Marshal: High-ranking military leader coordinating forces
    * Warlord: Independent military leader commanding personal armies
    * Commander: Force leader directing battles and campaigns
* **Military: Specialists**
    * Mercenary: Hired sword fighting for gold
    * Scout: Forward observer gathering intelligence
    * Ranger: Wilderness warrior tracking and surviving
    * Siege Engineer: Fortification specialist operating war machines
    * Sapper: Underground combat engineer digging and demolition
    * Artillerist: Siege weapon operator managing cannons and catapults
    * Quartermaster: See Commerce (manages military supplies)

* **Governance: Royalty**
    * King: Male sovereign ruler of kingdom
    * Queen: Female sovereign ruler or king's wife
    * Prince: Male royal heir or noble family member
    * Princess: Female royal heir or noble family member
    * Emperor: Supreme ruler of empire spanning multiple kingdoms
    * Empress: Female supreme ruler or emperor's wife
    * Archduke: High-ranking noble below royalty
    * Regent: Temporary ruler during monarch's absence
* **Governance: Nobility**
    * Duke: High noble governing duchy
    * Duchess: Female duke or duke's wife
    * Count: Mid-tier noble governing county
    * Countess: Female count or count's wife
    * Baron: Low-tier noble governing barony
    * Baroness: Female baron or baron's wife
    * Lord: General noble title with land rights
    * Lady: Female noble or lord's wife
    * Squire: Noble youth training for knighthood
* **Governance: Officials**
    * Chancellor: Chief advisor managing administration
    * Seneschal: Estate manager overseeing noble household
    * Steward: Property administrator managing lands
    * Chamberlain: Royal household manager
    * Herald: Official announcer and messenger
    * Diplomat: Foreign relations negotiator
    * Ambassador: Permanent diplomatic representative
    * Envoy: Temporary diplomatic messenger
    * Magistrate: Local judge presiding over legal matters
    * Judge: Legal authority determining verdicts
    * Bailiff: Court officer enforcing judgments
    * Constable: Law enforcement chief in region
    * Sheriff: Regional law officer with arrest powers
    * Mayor: Elected town leader managing local government
    * Council Member: Governing body representative
    * Bureaucrat: Administrative worker processing documentation
    * Clerk: Record keeper and document filer
    * Notary: Legal document authenticator

* **Guild: Leadership**
    * Guild Master: Organization leader directing guild operations
    * Guild Officer: Senior member managing guild sections
    * Guild Treasurer: Financial manager handling guild funds
    * Guild Recruiter: Membership coordinator bringing new members
    * Master Craftsman: Highest skill tier artisan
* **Guild: Members**
    * Journeyman: Qualified craftsman between apprentice and master
    * Apprentice: Trainee learning craft from master
    * Novice: Beginning guild member with basic skills
    * Artisan: Skilled independent craftsperson
    * Specialist: Expert focusing on specific techniques
* **Guild: Types**

    * Adventurers' Guild Master: Quest coordinator and bounty manager
