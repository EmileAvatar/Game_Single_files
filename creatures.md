# Creatures

## Creature Groups

* **Humanoid**: Bipedal intelligent beings with human-like form
* **Beast**: Natural animals and wild creatures
* **Undead**: Reanimated corpses and death-touched beings
* **Demon**: Malevolent entities from infernal realms
* **Dragon**: Ancient reptilian creatures of immense power
* **Elemental**: Living manifestations of natural forces
* **Construct**: Artificially created beings and animated objects
* **Aberration**: Twisted unnatural creatures defying normal biology
* **Giant**: Massive humanoids of extraordinary size
* **Insect**: Chitinous arthropods and bug-like creatures
* **Reptile**: Cold-blooded scaled creatures
* **Avian**: Bird-like flying creatures
* **Aquatic**: Water-dwelling beings
* **Plant**: Animate vegetation and fungal creatures
* **Ethereal**: Incorporeal spirit beings
* **Chimera**: Hybrid creatures combining multiple animal parts
* **Fey**: Magical beings from nature realms
* **Slime**: Amorphous ooze creatures that dissolve and absorb
* **Colossal**: Titan-sized legendary beasts of world-shaking power
