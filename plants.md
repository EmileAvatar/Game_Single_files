# Plants

## Plant Groups

* **Herbs**: Small medicinal and culinary plants
* **Flowers**: Blooming plants for beauty and alchemy
* **Trees**: Large woody plants for lumber and resources
* **Fungi**: Mushrooms and molds for food and alchemy
* **Crops**: Cultivated plants for food production
* **Magical Plants**: Rare flora with supernatural properties

---

## Plant List

* **Herbs: Medicinal**
    * Healing Herb: Common leafy plant used for basic healing remedies
    * Silverleaf: Pale herb treating poison and disease
    * Blood Root: Red-stemmed plant stanching wounds and bleeding
    * Feverfew: Flowering herb reducing illness and fever
    * Comfrey: Broad-leafed herb accelerating bone healing
    * Yarrow: Feathery herb with antiseptic properties
    * Valerian Root: Calming herb treating anxiety and sleep
* **Herbs: Culinary**
    * Sage: Aromatic herb for cooking and preservation
    * Thyme: Small-leafed herb adding flavor to meals
    * Rosemary: Needle-leafed herb for seasoning and memory
    * Basil: Fragrant herb for cooking and freshness
    * Mint: Cooling herb for drinks and digestion
    * Oregano: Savory herb for flavoring dishes
* **Herbs: Magical**
    * Mana Thistle: Purple flowering plant restoring magical energy
    * Witchgrass: Silvery grass amplifying spell effects
    * Ghost Weed: Translucent herb for spirit communication
    * Bloodthorn: Spiky plant enhancing blood magic
    * Dreamleaf: Soft herb inducing visions and prophecy

* **Flowers: Common**
    * Rose: Classic red flower for love potions and decoration
    * Daisy: Simple white flower with minor healing properties
    * Tulip: Colorful bloom for dyes and decoration
    * Sunflower: Large yellow flower producing edible seeds
    * Violet: Small purple flower for mild perfume
    * Lavender: Fragrant purple flower calming nerves
    * Jasmine: White flower with sweet scent for perfume
* **Flowers: Rare**
    * Moonbloom: White flower blooming only under moonlight
    * Blood Rose: Deep crimson flower for powerful potions
    * Frost Lily: Icy blue flower from frozen regions
    * Golden Lotus: Rare yellow flower with life extension properties
    * Star Orchid: Luminous flower connecting to celestial magic
* **Flowers: Elemental**
    * Fire Lily: Red-orange flower warm to the touch
    * Ice Blossom: Blue flower cold to the touch
    * Storm Petal: Purple flower crackling with static
    * Earth Bloom: Brown flower with stone-like petals

* **Trees: Common**
    * Oak: Sturdy hardwood tree for construction and fuel
    * Pine: Evergreen conifer producing resin and lumber
    * Birch: White-barked tree with flexible wood
    * Maple: Broad-leafed tree producing sweet sap
    * Ash: Strong wood for weapons and tools
    * Elm: Durable hardwood for construction
    * Fir: Tall conifer with strong straight timber
    * Cedar: Aromatic wood resistant to decay
* **Trees: Fruit**
    * Apple Tree: Common fruit tree producing edible apples
    * Pear Tree: Fruit tree bearing sweet pears
    * Cherry Tree: Flowering tree with small red fruits
    * Peach Tree: Fruit tree with fuzzy sweet fruit
    * Orange Tree: Citrus tree producing vitamin-rich fruit
    * Lemon Tree: Sour citrus tree for cooking and alchemy
* **Trees: Rare**
    * Ironwood: Dense dark wood incredibly strong
    * Silverwood: Pale magical wood conducting enchantments
    * Heartwood: Living tree with medicinal bark
    * Blackwood: Dark sinister wood for necromantic items
    * Ancient Oak: Centuries-old tree with wisdom essence
* **Trees: Magical**
    * Whispering Willow: Graceful tree whose leaves whisper secrets
    * Moonwood: Glowing tree absorbing lunar energy
    * Sunwood: Golden tree radiating warmth and light
    * World Tree Sapling: Rare offspring of mythical world tree
    * Spirit Tree: Ghostly white tree housing nature spirits

* **Fungi: Edible**
    * Button Mushroom: Common white mushroom for cooking
    * Portobello: Large meaty mushroom for hearty meals
    * Chanterelle: Golden trumpet-shaped delicacy
    * Morel: Honeycomb-textured gourmet mushroom
    * Truffle: Underground fungus highly prized for flavor
* **Fungi: Glowing**
    * Glowcap: Small mushroom emitting soft blue light
    * Moonshroom: Pale fungus glowing under moonlight
    * Fire Cap: Orange mushroom with warm glow
    * Ghost Mushroom: Translucent white glowing fungus
* **Fungi: Poisonous**
    * Death Cap: Deadly pale mushroom with lethal toxin
    * Red Toadstool: Spotted mushroom causing hallucinations
    * Widow's Hood: Black mushroom with paralytic poison
    * Bile Fungus: Foul mushroom inducing sickness
* **Fungi: Magical**
    * Mind Spore: Purple mushroom affecting consciousness
    * Time Fungus: Rare mushroom slowing perception of time
    * Spore Bloom: Explosive fungus releasing magical spores
    * Mycelium Network: Underground fungal network connecting plants

* **Crops: Grains**
    * Wheat: Staple grain for bread and flour
    * Barley: Hardy grain for brewing and food
    * Oats: Nutritious grain for porridge and feed
    * Rye: Dark grain for dense bread
    * Rice: Water-grown grain staple food
    * Corn: Tall grain producing large edible kernels
* **Crops: Vegetables**
    * Potato: Underground tuber vegetable
    * Carrot: Orange root vegetable
    * Cabbage: Leafy green vegetable head
    * Onion: Layered bulb vegetable for flavoring
    * Tomato: Red fruit vegetable for cooking
    * Lettuce: Leafy green for salads

    * Turnip: Round root vegetable for storage

* **Crops: Legumes**
    * Beans: Protein-rich pod vegetables
    * Peas: Small round seeds in pods
    * Lentils: Small lens-shaped legumes

* **Magical Plants: Rare**
    * Dragon Root: Fiery red plant growing near dragon lairs
    * Angel's Tear: Crystalline plant with divine essence
    * Demon Weed: Dark twisted plant from infernal regions
