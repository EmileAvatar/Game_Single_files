# Research

## Research Groups

* **Crafting**: Unlocking new recipes and techniques
* **Magic**: Discovering spells and enchantments
* **Biology**: Understanding creatures and anatomy
* **Architecture**: Learning construction methods
* **Combat**: Developing fighting techniques
* **Alchemy**: Mastering potion and compound creation

---

## Research List

* **Crafting: Metalworking**
    * Steel Forging: Unlocks steel weapon and armor recipes
    * Advanced Alloys: Enables mithril and adamantite crafting
    * Rune Inscription: Allows magical metal enhancement
    * Heavy Forging: Unlocks construction of large metal items
* **Crafting: Woodworking**
    * Fine Carpentry: Enables quality furniture construction
    * Siege Engineering: Unlocks defensive structure building
    * Bowcraft Mastery: Improves ranged weapon creation
* **Magic: Spell Schools**
    * Elemental Mastery: Unlocks advanced fire, ice, lightning spells
    * Necromantic Arts: Enables death magic and undead summoning
    * Divination Studies: Reveals detection and scrying spells
    * Transmutation Theory: Allows polymorph and alteration magic
* **Magic: Enchanting**
    * Basic Enchantment: Unlocks simple item enhancement
    * Advanced Enchanting: Enables powerful artifact creation
    * Soul Binding: Allows essence trapping in items
    * Permanent Enhancement: Creates lasting magical effects
* **Biology: Creature Studies**
    * Anatomy Knowledge: Improves organic part harvesting
    * Beast Training: Enables creature taming and control
    * Genetic Manipulation: Unlocks creature modification
    * Hybrid Creation: Allows combining creature traits
* **Biology: Monster Lore**
    * Dragon Biology: Reveals dragon weaknesses and harvesting
    * Undead Physiology: Understands necromantic creatures
    * Elemental Nature: Studies living elemental beings
    * Aberration Science: Analyzes unnatural creatures
* **Architecture: Construction**
    * Masonry Techniques: Unlocks stone building methods
    * Fortification Design: Enables defensive structures
    * Underground Engineering: Allows subterranean construction
    * Magical Architecture: Permits enchanted building materials
* **Architecture: Infrastructure**
    * Plumbing Systems: Enables water management structures
    * Road Networks: Unlocks paved pathway construction
    * Bridge Building: Allows spanning gaps and rivers
* **Combat: Weapons**
    * Weapon Specialization: Improves specific weapon effectiveness
    * Dual Wielding: Enables two-weapon fighting
    * Ranged Tactics: Enhances bow and crossbow usage
    * Polearm Mastery: Improves reach weapon techniques
* **Combat: Tactics**
    * Formation Fighting: Unlocks coordinated party bonuses
    * Ambush Techniques: Enables surprise attack advantages
    * Defensive Stance: Allows improved blocking and dodging
* **Alchemy: Potions**
    * Advanced Brewing: Unlocks powerful potion recipes
    * Poison Crafting: Enables toxin and venom creation
    * Elixir Mastery: Creates permanent enhancement draughts
    * Explosive Compounds: Allows alchemical bomb making
* **Alchemy: Transmutation**
    * Material Conversion: Enables ore to metal transformation
    * Essence Extraction: Allows harvesting magical energy
    * Purification Process: Removes impurities from materials
