# Rank System

## Japanese Ranking System (S to F)

Used for rating item quality, creature power, quest difficulty, player achievements, and overall progression tiers.

---

## Rank Tiers

* **S Rank (Supreme)**
    * Legendary tier, exceptionally rare and powerful
    * Reserved for the absolute best items, creatures, and achievements
    * Often unique or one-of-a-kind
    * Represents pinnacle performance and mastery
    * Visual Color: Gold/Rainbow

* **A Rank (Excellent)**
    * Superior quality, very powerful and reliable
    * High-end tier for endgame content
    * Rare but obtainable with significant effort
    * Represents exceptional skill and dedication
    * Visual Color: Purple/Violet

* **B Rank (Good)**
    * Above average quality, solid and dependable
    * Mid-to-late game tier
    * Uncommon rarity
    * Represents competent skill and progression
    * Visual Color: Blue

* **C Rank (Average)**
    * Standard quality, functional and adequate
    * Mid-game tier
    * Common rarity
    * Represents baseline competence
    * Visual Color: Green

* **D Rank (Below Average)**
    * Lower quality, functional but suboptimal
    * Early-to-mid game tier
    * Very common
    * Represents novice level
    * Visual Color: Yellow

* **E Rank (Poor)**
    * Weak quality, barely functional
    * Early game or starter tier
    * Extremely common
    * Represents beginner level
    * Visual Color: Orange

* **F Rank (Failure)**
    * Trash tier, broken or worthless
    * No practical value beyond salvage or disposal
    * Failure state or punishment tier
    * Represents critical failure
    * Visual Color: Red/Gray

## Application Examples

### Items & Equipment
- **S Rank**: Legendary Dragonslayer Sword, Ichor-Infused Armor
- **A Rank**: Adamantite Weapons, Masterwork Enchanted Gear
- **B Rank**: Mithril Equipment, Greater Enchanted Items
- **C Rank**: Steel Weapons, Standard Enchanted Gear
- **D Rank**: Iron Equipment, Minor Enchantments
- **E Rank**: Basic Weapons, Worn Armor
- **F Rank**: Broken Sword, Rusted Shield

### Creatures & Monsters
- **S Rank**: Ancient Dragons, Colossal Titans, Demon Lords
- **A Rank**: Adult Dragons, Greater Demons, Legendary Beasts
- **B Rank**: Young Dragons, Liches, Powerful Elementals
- **C Rank**: Wyverns, Trolls, Intermediate Monsters
- **D Rank**: Dire Wolves, Ogres, Basic Magic Creatures
- **E Rank**: Wolves, Goblins, Common Wildlife
- **F Rank**: Rats, Chickens, Harmless Critters

### Quests & Missions
- **S Rank**: Save the World, Defeat God-Tier Enemy
- **A Rank**: Slay Ancient Dragon, Stop Demon Invasion
- **B Rank**: Clear Legendary Dungeon, Defeat Boss
- **C Rank**: Escort Caravan, Hunt Dangerous Beast
- **D Rank**: Gather Resources, Clear Bandits
- **E Rank**: Deliver Package, Collect Herbs
- **F Rank**: Tutorial Missions, Fetch Quests

### Crafting Quality
- **S Rank**: Legendary Crafted (Divine Essence used)
- **A Rank**: Masterwork (+50% stats, special properties)
- **B Rank**: Superior (+35% stats, extra enchant slots)
- **C Rank**: Fine (+20% stats, improved durability)
- **D Rank**: Normal (standard stats)
- **E Rank**: Poor (reduced stats, -25%)
- **F Rank**: Broken (unusable, salvage only)

### Player Skill Level
- **S Rank**: Grandmaster (Skill 91-100)
- **A Rank**: Master (Skill 71-90)
- **B Rank**: Expert (Skill 51-70)
- **C Rank**: Journeyman (Skill 31-50)
- **D Rank**: Apprentice (Skill 11-30)
- **E Rank**: Novice (Skill 1-10)
- **F Rank**: Untrained (Skill 0)

### Alchemical Essences
- **S Rank**: Perfected Essence (Divine-tier, Ichor-based)
- **A Rank**: Refined Essence (2× power, enhanced)
- **B Rank**: Greater Essence (Enhanced quality)
- **C Rank**: Standard Essence (Base crafting tier)
- **D Rank**: Diluted Essence (Weak potency)
- **E Rank**: Impure Essence (Unstable, risky)
- **F Rank**: Failed Essence (Worthless byproduct)

### Enemy Difficulty
- **S Rank**: World Boss, Raid-Tier Enemies
- **A Rank**: Elite Boss, Major Antagonist
- **B Rank**: Mini-Boss, Champion Enemy
- **C Rank**: Veteran Enemy, Strong Foe
- **D Rank**: Standard Enemy, Common Threat
- **E Rank**: Weak Enemy, Trash Mob
- **F Rank**: Harmless, Non-Combatant

### Dungeon Difficulty
- **S Rank**: Legendary Ruins, Godly Trials
- **A Rank**: Ancient Tombs, Dragon Lairs
- **B Rank**: Fortified Strongholds, Dark Temples
- **C Rank**: Caves, Ruins, Standard Dungeons
- **D Rank**: Bandit Camps, Small Crypts
- **E Rank**: Cellars, Shallow Caves
- **F Rank**: Tutorial Areas, Safe Zones

### Achievements
- **S Rank**: Platinum Achievements (1% rarity)
- **A Rank**: Gold Achievements (5% rarity)
- **B Rank**: Silver Achievements (15% rarity)
- **C Rank**: Bronze Achievements (40% rarity)
- **D Rank**: Common Achievements (70% rarity)
- **E Rank**: Tutorial Achievements (95% rarity)
- **F Rank**: Participation Achievements (100% rarity)

### Faction Reputation
- **S Rank**: Exalted/Legendary Hero
- **A Rank**: Revered/Champion
- **B Rank**: Honored/Ally
- **C Rank**: Friendly/Respected
- **D Rank**: Neutral/Known
- **E Rank**: Unfriendly/Suspicious
- **F Rank**: Hostile/Enemy

## Visual Representation

Ranks are typically displayed with:
- **Colored borders** around items/icons
- **Star ratings** (F=0 stars, E=1, D=2, C=3, B=4, A=5, S=6)
- **Text labels** with rank letter
- **Glow effects** (S rank glows/shimmers)
- **Background colors** matching rank tier
- **Particle effects** for higher ranks

---

## Progression Notes

- Most players spend majority of time in **D-C-B** ranks (leveling phase)
- **A Rank** represents endgame competency
- **S Rank** is aspirational goal requiring mastery
- **E-F Ranks** primarily for early game or failure states
- Rank gaps matter: S rank item is significantly better than A rank
- Ranks create clear goals and reward progression
