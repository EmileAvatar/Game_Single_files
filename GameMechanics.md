# Game Mechanics

## Mechanic Groups

* **Combat**: Battle and conflict systems
* **Character**: Player development and customization
* **Exploration**: World navigation and discovery
* **Crafting**: Item creation and enhancement
* **Alchemy**: Three-tier potion and essence creation
* **Magic**: Spell construction and casting systems
* **Bioforging**: Creature creation and modification
* **Social**: NPC interaction and reputation
* **Economy**: Resource management and trade
* **Base Building**: Settlement construction and management

---

## Mechanic List

* **Combat: Core**
    * Turn-Based Combat: Sequential action resolution
    * Action Points: Limited actions per turn
    * Hit Chance: Accuracy-based attack resolution
    * Critical Hits: Bonus damage on lucky strikes
    * Flanking: Positional advantage bonuses
* **Combat: Advanced**
    * Area of Effect: Multi-target attacks
    * Status Effects: Temporary condition modifiers
    * Resistances: Damage type mitigation
    * Counter Attacks: Automatic retaliation strikes
    * Formation Tactics: Party positioning strategies
* **Character: Progression**
    * Experience Points: Combat and quest rewards
    * Leveling System: Character advancement tiers
    * Skill Trees: Branching ability specializations
    * Attribute Points: Stat allocation on level up
    * Class System: Role-based character types
* **Character: Customization**
    * Equipment Slots: Gear worn simultaneously
    * Talent Selection: Passive ability choices
    * Spell Learning: Magic acquisition system
    * Reputation Alignment: Faction standing effects
    * Character Creation: Initial build options
* **Exploration: Movement**
    * Grid-Based Movement: Tile-to-tile navigation
    * Overworld Travel: Fast map-to-map transit
    * Hidden Passages: Secret discoverable routes
    * Terrain Effects: Movement cost modifiers
    * Climbing/Swimming: Environmental traversal
* **Exploration: Discovery**
    * Fog of War: Unexplored area concealment
    * Trap Detection: Hidden hazard revelation
    * Lore Finding: World-building text discoveries
    * Quest Markers: Objective location indicators
    * Random Encounters: Procedural event triggers
* **Crafting: Creation**
    * Recipe Learning: Blueprint acquisition
    * Material Gathering: Resource harvesting
    * Crafting Stations: Required equipment usage
    * Quality Tiers: Item rarity levels
    * Enchanting: Magical enhancement system
* **Crafting: Advanced**
    * Item Upgrading: Enhancement of existing gear
    * Disassembly: Breaking items for materials
    * Bulk Production: Mass crafting efficiency
    * Research Unlocks: Technology progression
    * Experimentation: Discovery through trial
    * Quality System: Normal to Legendary item tiers
    * Skill Progression: Crafting expertise leveling (0-100)
    * Station Requirements: Specialized workbenches needed
    * Critical Success: Chance for bonus quality or extra items
    * Material Efficiency: Reduced costs at higher skill levels

* **Alchemy: Three-Tier System**
    * Tier 1 - Extraction: Converting raw materials to base extracts
    * Tier 2 - Combination: Creating Alchemical Essences from reagents
    * Tier 3 - Finalization: Crafting potions, items, or spell components
    * Essence Types: 47 types across 7 categories (Elemental, Vital, Mental, Physical, Transformation, Necromantic, Divine)
    * Storage Requirements: Specialized containers by danger level
    * Processing Time: Minutes to hours based on complexity
    * Success Chance: Skill-based with explosion/failure risks
    * Recipe Discovery: Experimentation system for unknown recipes
* **Alchemy: Applications**
    * Potion Brewing: Health, mana, buffs, transmutation elixirs
    * Essence Refinement: Enhancing power by 50% through purification
    * Spell Components: High-level magic material requirements
    * Bioforging Fuel: Essences used in creature creation
    * Enchanting Materials: Alchemical essences for magic items
    * Lair Power Sources: Essence-powered crafting stations

* **Magic: Spell Construction**
    * Modular System: Build spells from component parts
    * Framework Selection: Damage, heal, buff, summon, utility templates
    * Element Choice: Fire, ice, lightning, earth, poison, holy, shadow
    * Delivery Method: Projectile, area, touch, self, ray
    * Area of Effect: Size selection (single, 2x2, 3x3, 5x5, global)
    * Duration Modification: Instant, short, long, permanent
    * Power Scaling: Mana cost increases with intensity
    * Spell Level: Auto-calculated based on components (0-9)
    * Inscription: Requires Alchemical Essences to create scrolls
    * Spellbook Storage: Save and manage custom spells
* **Magic: Casting System**
    * Mana Cost: Tiered by spell level (1 mana for cantrips, 1000+ for level 9)
    * Magical Essences: Higher spells require Refined/Pure Mana or Ichor
    * Power Sources: Using specific essences grants bonuses (+25% elemental match)
    * Cooldowns: Time between spell uses
    * Casting Time: Instant, action, or ritual (minutes to hours)
    * Concentration: Maintaining active spell effects
    * Counter-Spelling: Interrupt enemy casting
    * Spell Failure: Chance based on armor worn and skill level

* **Bioforging: Creature Creation**
    * Template System: Base creature blueprints (3-15 parts)
    * Part Selection: Brain, eyes, body, limbs, wings, tail, organs, hide
    * Compatibility System: Part synergy affects success rate
    * Essence Requirements: Life Essence, Soul Essence, Mana consumed
    * Growth Time: 1-21 days in Creation Vat or Vitae Incubator
    * Mutation Risk: 0.5%-35% chance based on skill and parts
    * Stat Preview: See HP, damage, speed, abilities before creation
    * Blueprint Archive: Save successful designs for replication
* **Bioforging: Enhancement**
    * Gene Splicing: Combine traits from multiple organisms
    * Evolution Acceleration: Reduce growth time by 80% (5x essence cost)
    * Intentional Mutation: Use Chaos Essence for controlled changes
    * Hybrid Creation: Chimeras with mixed animal features
    * Creature Tiers: Common to Legendary based on complexity
    * Loyalty System: Creature obedience and control mechanics
    * Command Interface: Direct creature actions in combat
    * Inherited Traits: Using specific blood types grants abilities
* **Social: Interaction**
    * Dialogue Trees: Branching conversation choices
    * Persuasion Checks: Charisma-based outcomes
    * Quest Giving: NPC mission assignment
    * Companion Recruitment: Party member joining
    * Trade Bartering: NPC buying and selling
* **Social: Reputation**
    * Faction Standing: Group relationship tracking
    * Karma System: Moral choice consequences
    * Fame/Infamy: Public recognition levels
    * Hostility Triggers: Reputation-based combat
    * Reward Scaling: Standing-based quest outcomes
* **Economy: Resources**
    * Currency System: Gold and money management
    * Inventory Weight: Carrying capacity limits
    * Resource Nodes: Respawning material sources
    * Scarcity Mechanics: Limited availability items
    * Storage Systems: Item organization facilities
* **Economy: Trading**
    * Shop Inventories: Merchant stock rotation
    * Price Fluctuation: Dynamic market values
    * Bulk Discounts: Volume purchasing benefits
    * Stolen Goods: Illegal item fencing
    * Auction System: Bidding mechanics
* **Base Building: Construction**
    * Tile Placement: Manual structure building
    * Building Blueprints: Premade structure templates
    * Resource Requirements: Material costs for construction
    * Upgrade Paths: Improvement progression
    * Zone Designation: Area purpose assignment
* **Base Building: Management**
    * NPC Workers: Automated task assignment
    * Defense Systems: Protection mechanisms
    * Resource Generation: Production facilities
    * Research Labs: Technology advancement buildings
    * Population Growth: Settlement expansion mechanics
* **Base Building: Power Systems**
    * Mana Reservoirs: Store 1000-10,000 Mana for crafting stations
    * Essence Generators: Life/Soul Essence cultivation from organic matter
    * Power Networks: Conduits distributing energy to workstations
    * Celestial Collectors: Harvest Moon Light and Star Dust at night
    * Divine Altars: Convert offerings to Ichor and Divine Spark
    * Automatic Distribution: Power priority system during shortages
    * Recharge Rates: Passive mana generation (10-500 per hour)
    * Overclocking: +50% station speed at 2x power consumption
