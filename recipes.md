# Recipes

## Recipe Groups

* **Weapons**: Crafting armaments for combat
* **Armor**: Creating protective equipment
* **Consumables**: Making single-use items
* **Buildings**: Constructing structures and tiles
* **Furniture**: Crafting interior objects
* **Tools**: Making utility items
* **Magic Items**: Creating enchanted objects

---
